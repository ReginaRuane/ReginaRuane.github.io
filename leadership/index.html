Roles

2022 –

Managing Director

Computational Social Science Lab & Center for AI and Society

University of Pennsylvania

A joint initiative of the School of Engineering and Applied Science, the Wharton School, and the Annenberg School for Communication. Direct research and administrative operations, budgets, staffing, and strategic development; serve on the leadership team of the Center for AI and Society, building interdisciplinary collaborations across analytics, artificial intelligence, and their social consequences.

  • Co-led the establishment of the Penn Center on Media, Technology, and Democracy, spanning six schools.
  • Convened AI for Disaster Relief with Meta AI for Good and Crisis Ready, connecting agencies, nonprofits, industry, and researchers.
  • Built the cross-school relationships — six deans, the President's office, finance, communications, development — that a grant of that size requires.
2022 –

Executive Director

International Society for Computational Social Science

ISCSS / IC2S2

Oversee operations and organizational structure for the Society and for IC2S2, the field's flagship international conference. Coordinate with international collaborators, academic institutions, and industry partners, and advise the board on governance and strategic direction.

2020 – 2022

Executive Director

Data Science Institute

Temple University

Led a university-wide institute: advisory board and governance, partnerships, budgets, faculty and research engagement, grants, and outreach. Concurrently Assistant Professor of Research in Statistical Science and Managing Director of Pedagogical Research.

  • Built partnerships with Disney, Wells Fargo, Google, BlackRock, Millennium Management, LongTail Alpha, Blockchain.com, the Keystone Space Collaborative, and the City of Philadelphia.
  • Developed and oversaw NSF, NIH, Office of Naval Research, DARPA, and NASA grant projects.
  • Founded a Women in Data Science Collective at Temple.
2018 – 2020

Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Harvard University & Temple University

Statistical training under Edoardo M. Airoldi and Paul A. Pavlou: design and analysis of digital experiments, sampling and inference with network data, and causal analysis in complex observational studies with time, space, or network structure.
